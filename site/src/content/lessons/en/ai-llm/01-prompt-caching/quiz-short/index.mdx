The TTL choice does not change the per-token read discount (always 0.1x); it only decides whether the entry survives between reads. The 1-hour tier (2x write instead of 1.25x) earns its premium when traffic is bursty or low-volume — gaps longer than 5 minutes but shorter than an hour — so a 5-minute entry would expire in the gap and each burst would pay a fresh write.
