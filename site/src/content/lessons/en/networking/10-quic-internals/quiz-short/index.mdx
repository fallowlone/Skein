QUIC moves multiplexing into the transport: each stream has its own ordering, flow-control window, and loss detection, so a lost STREAM frame only stalls its own stream. All stream, flow-control, and congestion state survive. During validation the server may send at most 3x the bytes it received (anti-amplification), so a spoofed source address cannot turn the server into a reflection amplifier. Four encryption levels — Initial (deterministic from the Connection ID, for integrity not secrecy), 0-RTT (from a cached session ticket), Handshake (from the DH exchange), and 1-RTT (final keys) — map to three packet-number spaces (Initial, Handshake, Application).
