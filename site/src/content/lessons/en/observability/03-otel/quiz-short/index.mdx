Tail sampling runs in the Collector after the full trace has arrived, so it can keep 100% of error traces and all traces over a latency threshold, plus a probabilistic baseline; but it is stateful and expensive, buffering every span in RAM for the decision window. Production combination: ParentBased(TraceIdRatioBased(0.2)) at the SDK to cut buffer volume ~80% and avoid partial traces, plus tail sampling at the gateway keeping 100% errors + 100% slow (>1s) + ~1% baseline — net ~3-5% of volume retained, 100% of interesting traffic captured." },
